About The Artist

The name's Lois. I'm an 19 year old Filipino Artist and Animator. My art style is quite varied, ranging from a shapey cartoony style to a semi-realistic anime style. Also, I like having a more sketchy, chalky and messy art style compared to a more clean one. I just let myself be flexible and have fun with my art, not letting myself be restricted with what's considered the "art norm". I mostly specialize in Character Design and 2D Character Animation.
